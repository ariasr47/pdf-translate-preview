# Authority-specific use of translated documents

A translated derivative is not automatically an official edition or a certified
translation. State its provenance and actual review status. Scripts do not
supply a human translator's certification or make signatures on changed PDF
bytes valid.

Determine intended use before making a filing claim. Check the receiving
authority's current instructions for the specific program, document type,
language and jurisdiction. Cite that source and date in the job brief. Some
processes require an official-language form; others require a translation plus
the original and particular translator declarations. There is no universal
rule that every translated form must be filed only in English or its source
language.

For example, [IRCC's super visa document guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/parent-grandparent-super-visa/forms-documents.html)
contains program-specific translation requirements. Do not generalize them to
other Canadian programs, countries or private documents. Recheck authoritative
instructions at the time of the job.

If a notice is needed, use neutral, accurate wording such as:

> Unofficial translation of [title/version] into [locale]. Prepared from the
> identified source for [purpose]. Review status: [actual status]. Consult the
> receiving authority's instructions before submission.

Adapt it to the target language and verified circumstances. Do not invent a
claim that the source-language version always governs. Prefer a delivery note;
add in-document text only when the user's purpose requires it and there is
reviewed room. Never obscure original text, shrink substantive content merely
to insert a disclaimer, or change page count without authorization.

For consequential consent, court, tax or immigration use, distinguish automated
checks, model review and qualified human revision. Report unavailable review
honestly. This guide is workflow guidance, not legal advice or certification.
